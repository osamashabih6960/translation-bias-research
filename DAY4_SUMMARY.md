
# Day 4 — Translation Bias Pilot Evaluation

## Objective
Prepare an initial evaluation workflow for Bengali-to-English translation pairs.

## Completed Tasks
1. Defined the research question and hypotheses.
2. Documented evaluation criteria.
3. Prepared a 10-pair pilot evaluation CSV.
4. Calculated Bengali and English word counts.
5. Added provisional meaning and gender labels.
6. Verified the labels and generated a pilot report.

## Pilot Results
- Total pairs: 10
- Provisional meaning score 2: 4
- Provisional meaning score 1: 1
- Meaning score U: 5
- Provisional gender label 0: 3
- Gender label U: 7

## Output Files
- DAY4_RESEARCH_PLAN.md
- DAY4_EVALUATION_CRITERIA.md
- data/processed/pilot_evaluation_10.csv
- data/processed/pilot_evaluation_summary.csv
- data/processed/pilot_evaluation_labeled.csv
- data/processed/day4_pilot_report.txt

## Limitations
- Labels are provisional and have not been independently validated.
- Several pairs need additional context.
- The pilot contains only 10 examples.
- No confirmed translation-bias rate has been established.
- Results cannot be generalized to the full dataset.

## Next Steps
- Validate labels against fuller source context.
- Establish a reproducible sampling strategy.
- Evaluate a larger sample.
- Record evidence for every confirmed issue.
